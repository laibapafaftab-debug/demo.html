The difference automation makes
Without automation
  • Lead sits in inbox for hours, sometimes days
  • Manually reading and prioritizing every message
  • Copy-pasting details into a spreadsheet
  • Follow-up gets forgotten during busy days
Response time: 2–24 hours
With automation
  • Lead is captured and read the instant it arrives
  • AI classifies urgency automatically
  • CRM record created without touching a keyboard
  • Follow-up + booking link sent immediately
Response time: Under 60 seconds
